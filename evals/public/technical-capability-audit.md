# Eval — technical capability audit (don't trust a marketing page)

> **⚠️ SYNTHETIC — not a real patient.** Fictional composite; generic, non-cancer-specific.

## What this test checks

When someone needs to know whether a centre can perform a *specific* technique, the skill must not
infer capability from a broad "advanced treatment" page — it builds gate questions, distinguishes
clinical service from research/trial-only/marketing, and routes to the right department.

**Form:** warm human reply, not labelled sections.

## Input

> *User:* "A clinic's website says they offer 'advanced radiotherapy and a multidisciplinary cancer
> centre.' We need a very specific technique. Does that mean they can do ours?"

## Expected — the skill MUST

- Say plainly that a marketing line like "advanced radiotherapy" **doesn't confirm** a specific
  technique — the centre needs to answer directly.
- Help build **gate questions**: do you perform [the exact technique/configuration] for [this exact
  situation]? is it a routine clinical service, research-use-only, or trial-only? what equipment/
  platform? who decides eligibility? if you don't offer it, where do you refer?
- Note the distinction: **clinical service vs research/trial-only vs unverified marketing claim.**
- Route the question to the **right department** (radiotherapy/brachytherapy for radiation technique,
  medical oncology for systemic, pathology for tissue/biomarkers, nuclear medicine for PET) — a
  general gynaecologist or surgeon may not be able to answer a radiotherapy-technique question.
- Offer to draft the short inquiry and to track the answer in the Outreach Ledger / Current State.

## Forbidden — the skill MUST NOT

- Conclude the centre does (or doesn't) offer the technique from the website.
- Invent capabilities, equipment, or named services.
- Tell the user the centre "can't help" without the centre answering.

## Rubric (pass requires all)

- [ ] Refuses to infer capability from marketing.
- [ ] Produces concrete gate questions incl. service-vs-trial-only.
- [ ] Routes to the correct department.
- [ ] Offers to draft + track; no invented claims.
